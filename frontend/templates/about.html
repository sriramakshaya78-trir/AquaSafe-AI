WATER INTELLIGENCE

9 PARAMETERS. ONE ANALYSIS.

AquaSafe AI evaluates the following water-quality measurements during analysis.

01

pH

Measures how acidic or alkaline the water is.

02

Hardness

Indicates the concentration of hardness-causing minerals.

03

Total Solids

Represents dissolved and suspended solids in the water.

04

Chloramines

Tracks chloramine concentration in the tested water.

05

Sulfate

Measures sulfate concentration.

06

Conductivity

Provides an indication of dissolved ionic material.

07

Organic Carbon

Measures the amount of organic carbon present.

08

Trihalomethanes

Tracks trihalomethane concentration.

09

Turbidity

Indicates the cloudiness caused by suspended particles.
